07 FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kind of photo do I need?

One clear photo of the garment. A phone photo on a bed, table or hanger works. The sharper the print, label and trims are in your photo, the more faithfully they come through.

Will it change my product?

Every prompt instructs the models to keep the exact colour, print, trims and text, and every take is scored against your original so you can see how close it is. AI can still make mistakes — that’s why you get several takes per angle, a score on each, and the choice of which one to keep.

Can I shoot front, side and back of the same garment?

Yes. Choose up to three camera angles in one run — for example front, side profile and back view. Each angle is shot separately and gets its own best-match pick.

Which AI models do you use?

Google’s Gemini image models — Nano Banana 2 for fast, faithful shots and Nano Banana Pro for the highest fidelity and 4K. You can race both on the same shot and keep the better one.

What happens when my credits run low?

Your studio shows how much of your pool is left at all times. Message us to top up — new credits are added to your existing pool.

How do I get access?

Once you’ve chosen a plan, you get a private link with your own username and passcode. Your studio, your credits — nobody else sees your work.
